## Concept

Last seen on the runway at Prada FW13. Before that, on every architecture student of the autumn of 1973. Corduroy returns when the cultural mood favors texture over polish — and the mood has shifted. The 2026 iteration trades narrow wales for the eight-line, cuts that read as outerwear rather than chore, and a palette pulled from oxidized brass and deep moss rather than the dusty browns of the previous wave. Heavier. More tailored. Less apologetic.
